Across the fixture this yields exactly fourteen inputs, five of them user-caused. The first response spans two lines (a thinking block, then a tool call) and yields one dispatch.

Desired behaviour · usage

  • Only assistant lines report usage. A response is keyed by message.id, falling back to requestId. The synthetic model of error lines never counts. Absent counters are zero, and a usage that isn't an object is no usage at all.
  • A response split across lines counts once. For the fixture: claude-opus-5 has input 8, output 646, cache writes 34 013, cache reads 95 315. claude-fable-5-1 has output 80. No other models appear.
  • Usages add with +, += and sum().
  • Stretch. Prices parse from $6.25 / MTok, $0.50 and 5 into integer micro-dollars. At most six decimals, no sign, digits on both sides of the point, and no floats anywhere. Cost rounds down and saturates instead of overflowing. Rate lookup tries the exact model, then the model with a bracketed suffix such as [1m] removed. A transcript's cost reports the models it couldn't price.
